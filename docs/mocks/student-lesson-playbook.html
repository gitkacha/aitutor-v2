Balancing Number Sentences

⏱ 4 min read ◆ Number Sentences
✦

The idea

A number sentence is like a see-saw. Both sides have to weigh exactly the same. If you know one side, you can always work out the missing number on the other side.

1

Step by step

  1. Cover the missing number with your finger.
  2. Work out what the other side adds up to — that's your target.
  3. Do the opposite operation to get back to the missing number (+ undoes −, × undoes ÷).
  4. Put your answer back in and check both sides match.
⚡

Your trick

Show-off move

Don't guess numbers one by one. Ask "what's missing to reach the target?" and undo in one step. No trial and error — you'll finish these in seconds.

✓

Worked examples

7 + ▢ = 12
Target is 12. Undo the +7 → 12 − 7 = 5
✓ Check: 7 + 5 = 12
4 × ▢ = 20
Target is 20. Undo the ×4 → 20 ÷ 4 = 5
✓ Check: 4 × 5 = 20
▢ − 6 = 9
Target is 9. Undo the −6 → 9 + 6 = 15
✓ Check: 15 − 6 = 9
!

Gotchas the test setters hope you fall for

Doing the same operation instead of the opposite — adding when you should take away.
Forgetting to check both sides match at the end.
Rushing a two-step sentence like 3 + ▢ × 2 = 11 — do the × first.
